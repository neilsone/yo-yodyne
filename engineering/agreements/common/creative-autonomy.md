# Working Agreement: Creative Autonomy

**Scope:** All units
**Date:** 2026-03-08

---

## Purpose

Ensure that delegated creative work maintains artistic integrity and that producing units develop their own creative voice within the Yo-Yodyne brand.

## Standards

This agreement codifies the Creative Autonomy Principle defined in `openspec/specs/creative-autonomy/spec.md`.

### When Work Is Delegated

1. The **requesting unit** provides a brief specifying requirements: subject, format, dimensions, technical constraints, deadline, and any explicit creative direction.
2. The **producing unit** executes the work with complete creative control over all decisions not explicitly constrained in the brief.
3. If no creative direction is specified, the producing unit exercises its own judgment consistent with the Yo-Yodyne brand.

### What Reviewers May Assess

- Technical correctness
- Compliance with stated requirements
- Factual accuracy
- Format and dimension specifications

### What Reviewers May NOT Override

- Composition and layout choices
- Tone and voice
- Style and aesthetic approach
- Artistic judgment of any kind

### Disputes

1. The requesting and producing units attempt to resolve informally
2. If unresolved, escalate to the PI
3. The default resolution favors the producing unit's creative discretion

## Exceptions

- When a brief explicitly specifies creative constraints (e.g., "must use brand color palette"), those constraints are binding
- Brand-critical artifacts (logo, core identity elements) require PI approval regardless

## Review

Reviewed if creative disputes become frequent or the principle proves unclear in practice.
