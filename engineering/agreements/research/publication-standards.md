# Working Agreement: Publication Standards

**Scope:** Research unit
**Date:** 2026-03-08

---

## Purpose

Define quality standards for research publications, from informal treatments to technical papers.

## Standards

### Dual Audience Approach

Research publications SHALL support two audiences:

1. **Informal treatment** — Accessible to a less technical audience. Conceptual, narrative, engaging. Published to the labs site.
2. **Technical depth** — Equations, proofs, detailed methodology. Linked as references from the informal treatment.

Both may exist as a single document with clear delineation, or as separate linked documents.

### Quality Criteria

| Criterion | Informal | Technical |
|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Required | Required |
| Citations | Key references | Comprehensive |
| Equations | Avoided or explained intuitively | Full notation |
| Figures | Explanatory, engaging | Precise, labeled |
| Voice | Conversational, curious | Formal, precise |
| Length | Concise (engagement-focused) | As needed |

### Metadata

All publications use the artifact metadata standard with the addition of:
- `audience: general | technical | both`
- `abstract:` (required for technical, recommended for informal)
- `references:` (list of related technical papers or resources)

### Brand Voice

Publications represent the Yo-Yodyne brand. Even technical writing should feel considered and intentional — not dry or perfunctory. The wabi-sabi aesthetic extends to prose: imperfection in service of authenticity.

## Exceptions

- Internal notes and drafts are exempt from dual-audience requirements
- Experiment logs follow the experiment protocol, not publication standards

## Review

Reviewed after the first published research piece.
