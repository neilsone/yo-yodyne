# Working Agreement: Content Standards

**Scope:** Production unit
**Date:** 2026-03-08

---

## Purpose

Ensure all published content meets quality, brand, and engagement standards.

## Standards

### Brand Voice

Yo-Yodyne content is:
- **Refined** — Considered word choices, no filler
- **Curious** — Asks questions, invites exploration
- **Minimal** — Says enough, not more
- **Warm** — Accessible despite sophistication
- **Subtly playful** — An unexpected turn of phrase, a wry observation — never forced

### Content Quality

- Factual accuracy verified before publication
- Sources cited where claims are made
- Images and media are high quality, properly attributed
- Content is proofread (spelling, grammar, consistency)

### Accessibility

- Images have meaningful alt text
- Content is readable without JavaScript
- Color contrast meets WCAG AA minimum
- Semantic HTML structure

### Content Types

| Type | Standards |
|---|---|
| Research articles | Per publication standards (dual audience) |
| Experiment logs | Per experiment protocol |
| Project pages | Clear description, status, links to subdomain/repo |
| Creative pieces | Per creative autonomy — Production owns creative decisions |
| About/meta | Accurate, current, brand-consistent |

## Exceptions

- Alpha-channel content may be rougher; it's internal only
- Draft content in the staging pipeline is exempt

## Review

Reviewed quarterly or when content quality issues are identified.
