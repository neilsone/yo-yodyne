# Spec: Creative Autonomy Principle

**Status:** ACTIVE
**Date:** 2026-03-08

---

## Requirement: Creative Ownership

This is a core principle of Yo-Yodyne's organizational design.

#### Scenario: Delegated Creative Work
- GIVEN a task delegated from one unit to another for production
- WHEN the producing unit executes the work
- THEN the producing unit SHALL have complete creative control over the work product

#### Scenario: Requesting Unit Constraints
- GIVEN a creative brief or task request
- WHEN the requesting unit specifies requirements
- THEN the requesting unit MAY specify:
  - Subject matter
  - Dimensions and format
  - Technical constraints
  - Deadline
  - Explicit creative direction (if any)
- AND the requesting unit MUST NOT dictate creative decisions that are not explicitly specified in the brief

#### Scenario: Review Boundaries
- GIVEN a completed creative deliverable
- WHEN the requesting unit reviews the work
- THEN the requesting unit MAY assess:
  - Technical correctness
  - Compliance with stated requirements
  - Factual accuracy
- AND the requesting unit SHALL NOT override creative decisions including:
  - Composition and layout
  - Tone and voice
  - Style and aesthetic approach
  - Artistic judgment

#### Scenario: Unspecified Creative Direction
- GIVEN a task with no explicit creative direction
- WHEN the producing unit makes creative choices
- THEN those choices SHALL be made at the discretion of the producing unit
- AND those choices SHALL be consistent with the Yo-Yodyne brand identity

#### Scenario: Creative Disputes
- GIVEN a disagreement about creative direction
- WHEN the requesting and producing units cannot resolve it
- THEN the dispute SHALL escalate to the PI
- AND the default resolution SHALL favor the producing unit's creative discretion
